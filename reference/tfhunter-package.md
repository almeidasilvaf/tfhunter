# tfhunter: Identification and classification of eukaryotic transcription factors

tfhunter is used to identify transcription factors (TFs) from
whole-genome protein sequence data of any eukaryotic species, and
classify them into families using multiple classification schemes. TFs
are identified using pre-built hidden Markov model profiles for
DNA-binding domains. Then, auxiliary and forbidden domains are used with
DNA-binding domains to classify TFs into families and subfamilies (when
applicable). TFs from all eukaryotes can be classified with CIS-BP's
scheme, which defines families based on the DNA-binding domains of each
TF. Mammalian TFs can also be classified into the structure-based
superclasses, classes, and families of TFClass. Plant TFs can also be
classified with the schemes of PlantTFDB (58 families/subfamilies) and
TAPscan (over 130 families of transcription-associated proteins), and
into the structure-based families, classes, and superclasses of
Plant-TFClass. tfhunter is the successor of the Bioconductor package
planttfhunter.
